The Shutdown Sequence

What happens when RES engineers isolate your installation for servicing — every step verified, documented, and signed off by a Licensed Electrical Worker.

  1. 01

    Energised

    Normal operation. Load current flows from the incoming supply through the main breaker to your distribution boards.

  2. 02

    Isolate

    The main breaker is opened under a Permit-to-Work. Current stops — the installation is separated from the supply.

  3. 03

    Earth

    The earth switch is closed, discharging stored energy and holding the conductors safely at earth potential.

  4. 04

    Lock Out

    Locks and tags go on. Dead is verified with a calibrated tester. Only then does the work begin.
